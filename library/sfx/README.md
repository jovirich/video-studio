# SFX

Effects, foley, and ambience beds available to any studio on the platform.

**Maturity: DESIGNED.** Empty. No effect has been licensed or placed.

## What goes in here

Cross-studio sound: hard effects, foley elements, room tones, ambiences, transitions,
and utility design material. Subject-neutral, brand-neutral, reusable.

Not here: anything captured for one production and specific to it, anything recorded on
location as part of a line's work (that is a source record with its own consent and
access conditions), and anything that carries a place's identity strongly enough to be
an editorial claim.

That last one is not a stretch. A specific market ambience, a specific call to prayer,
a specific bird endemic to one region — these read as documentary evidence about a
place whether or not they were recorded there. A shared library is the wrong place for
them precisely because it makes them frictionless to reuse somewhere they are not
true. If it would make a claim about where a sequence is set, it belongs to a line.

## The licence requirement

Same as [music_beds](../music_beds/README.md), and it is the same trap:

**The grant must permit inclusion in a distributed finished film. "Royalty free" does
not answer that.** It is a payment model, not a scope. Core/02 §2 puts LUTs, plugins,
and stock SFX in one category for one reason — all three are bought casually, get baked
into the delivered work, and are almost never re-read before delivery.

The questions a row must answer:

| Question | Why it bites |
|---|---|
| Does the grant permit **synchronisation into an audiovisual work**? | Some libraries license listening, production use, and broadcast use at three different tiers. |
| Does it permit **broadcast and streaming** specifically? | Ask separately. It is frequently a separate grant. |
| Is the grant **per seat, per project, or platform-wide**? | A per-project grant used across four studios is a breach that looks exactly like normal use. |
| Does it permit **redistribution as part of the finished work**, and **sublicensing to a distributor**? | The distributor will require onward grant. |
| Does it prohibit **use in a competing library or as a standalone asset**? | Common, and relevant if the studio ever publishes stems, an M&E, or an asset pack. |
| **Attribution** — required, and in what exact wording? | Carried verbatim, per the clearance log. |
| Perpetual or **subscription**? | A lapsed subscription can affect material already delivered, depending on the grant. Record the expiry. |

Every effect or pack carries a row in
[../../rights/permissions/clearance_log.md](../../rights/permissions/clearance_log.md)
under category *LUTs, plugins, stock SFX*, with a `CLR-STUDIO-*` ID.

A **pack** may be one row where its licence is genuinely one grant covering the whole
pack — but the row must name the pack's boundary precisely, because packs grow by
update and the grant may not cover what arrived later.

## A note on generated SFX

Utility effects generated by a tool are permitted under core/01 §1 subject to the music
policy, and they are **not** cleared by this folder. A generated effect is cleared by
its vendor's terms, recorded in
[../../rights/permissions/model_terms_register.md](../../rights/permissions/model_terms_register.md),
and it carries a manifest entry with its prompt card and seed like any other generated
asset. Do not park generated audio here to avoid the manifest — the library is for
licensed third-party material, and the moment those two categories mix, neither register
describes reality.

## Naming

```
sfx_<class>_<slug>_v<NN>.wav
amb_<class>_<slug>_v<NN>.wav

sfx_impact_wood-heavy_v01.wav
amb_room_stone-interior-large_v02.wav
```

Lowercase, ASCII, hyphens within a field, underscores between fields, per
[../../standards/naming_conventions.md](../../standards/naming_conventions.md). The
`sfx` and `amb` class markers match the asset-name classes in that document.

The slug describes **the sound**, not where it came from or what it was used for.
`stone-interior-large` is right; `episode-two-cave` is wrong, and will be wrong again
the moment episode two is recut.

## Manifest and storage

Audio binaries are gitignored ([../../.gitignore](../../.gitignore)); the manifest is
not. Per item or pack: filename or pack name and boundary, SHA-256, duration, sample
rate and bit depth, whether the recording is mono or stereo, clearance ID, licence
scope, expiry, and attribution wording.

Record sample rate and bit depth per item. A library assembled at mixed rates is a
library that will be silently resampled by whichever tool touches it first, and the
resampling is not reversible.
