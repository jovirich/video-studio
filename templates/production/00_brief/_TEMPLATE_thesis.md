# Thesis — TBD — production working title

> Copy this file; do not fill it in place.

The thesis is the answer the evidence supports, written at the confidence the
evidence supports, together with what would show it to be wrong. It is expected to
change during research. What it may not do is change *silently*.

## 1. Statement

**TBD — one declarative sentence.**

Write it at the register the evidence supports, not the register that sounds best.
The registers are defined in the pack's editorial standards
([../../../packs/documentary-history/01_editorial_standards.md](../../../packs/documentary-history/01_editorial_standards.md)):

| Register | Use when |
|---|---|
| `established` | Independent sources agree and the point is not seriously disputed |
| `probable` | The weight of evidence points one way; a reasonable specialist could differ |
| `contested` | Specialists actively disagree, and both positions have standing |
| `inferred` | Not attested; reasoned from adjacent evidence by a chain a reviewer can attack |
| `traditional` | Held in a tradition, transmitted as such, presented as such |
| `unknown` | The honest answer, and a perfectly publishable one |

**Register of this thesis:** TBD — one of the above.

*Why the register is on the thesis and not just on the claims:* a production whose
claims are individually hedged and whose thesis is stated flatly has laundered its
uncertainty through structure. The hedge has to survive to the top level or it did
not happen.
