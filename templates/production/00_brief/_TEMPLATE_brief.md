> the failure this repository exists to prevent.

## 1. The question

**TBD — one sentence, ending in a question mark, with stakes.**

*Why this field exists:* a production is organised around a question or it is
organised around nothing. A topic ("the history of X") admits any material and
therefore selects none, and the absence of a spine is invisible on paper and
obvious in the assembly.

Test it: could the answer have gone the other way? If not, it is a subject heading.

**What is at stake in the answer:** TBD — who is affected by which answer is right,
and what a viewer understands differently depending on it.

## 4. Scope

**In scope:** TBD — the period, region, and actors this production covers.

**Explicitly out of scope:** TBD — what a reasonable viewer might expect and will
not get, and why.

*Why the second list exists:* an unstated exclusion reads as an omission or an
evasion. A stated one reads as a decision. The same absence, two different
readings, and only one of them is defensible.

**Comprehensiveness claim:** TBD — normally `none`. A production that claims to
cover everything invites exactly the criticism it is least able to answer.
