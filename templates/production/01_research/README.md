# 01_research

Where the brief's prediction meets the evidence. This stage produces the material
the script is permitted to draw on, and nothing else may enter the script later.

## The rule that shapes this folder

**Claims and source records live at the LINE, not here.**

`studios/<studio>/lines/<line>/sources/` and `.../research/` hold the registry. A
claim used by two productions is one record with two entries in its `used_in` list —
never two copies. Copying a claim into a production folder guarantees the copies
diverge, and the divergence is invisible until a fact-check catches it, or does not.

What lives here is this production's **working layer** over that registry.

## What goes here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `research_brief.md` | The scoped research assignment: what must be established, at what tier, by when |
| `claim_requirements.md` | Every point the outline needs, mapped to the claim that must exist before script lock |
| `source_coverage.md` | Which parts of the story are thin, and what would thicken them |
| `open_questions.md` | This production's slice of the line's `QST-` register, with status |
| `interviews/` | Interview plans, consent references, transcript pointers |
| `bibliography.md` | Generated by `studio_ops report bibliography` from the line registry — not hand-kept **[NOT BUILT]** |

## Before this stage starts

- **Greenlight is signed.** Research before greenlight is reading; research after it
  is a budgeted assignment with a lead accountable for it.
- The thesis from [00_brief](../00_brief/) is committed, so that what research
  disconfirms is on the record rather than reconstructed from memory.

## Before this stage can be left

The **source lock** gate is signed by the Research Lead, certifying:

1. **Every claim the outline requires exists as a claim record**, at the tier its
   confidence register demands.
2. **Independence has been checked on every `established` claim.** Two sources that
   share an upstream origin are one source. Independence is asserted deliberately,
   in the claim's `independent_of` field — it is never inferred from two different
   titles on a shelf.
3. **Contested claims carry named positions.** "Some historians" fails the gate.
4. **T4 oral sources have consent records** and follow the oral history protocol.
5. **Open questions are registered**, not carried in someone's head. An open
   question is a publishable state; an unregistered one is a landmine.

After source lock, the script may only draw on what is in the registry. That is the
whole point of the gate: it fixes the evidence *before* the writing, so the writing
cannot quietly determine the evidence.

## Model output is not a source

Generative tools are permitted here for locating material, summarising documents you
have also read, drafting translations, and structuring notes. Their output is **T5 —
never citable**. Every lead is verified against the actual document before it becomes
a claim. See [../../../core/01_provenance_and_ai_disclosure.md](../../../core/01_provenance_and_ai_disclosure.md) §1.

The failure this rule prevents is specific and easy: a model produces a fluent,
correctly formatted, entirely fictitious citation, and it survives because it looks
exactly like the real ones around it.
