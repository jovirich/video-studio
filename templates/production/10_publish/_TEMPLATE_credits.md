# Credits — TBD — production title

> Copy this file; do not fill it in place.

Credits are checked by the people named in them, which makes this the one document
in the production with a guaranteed expert audience. A misspelled name, an omitted
contributor, or a credit in a form somebody asked not to be used is not a
proofreading error; it is a broken commitment, and it is public.

## How each name gets here

Every credit traces to a record — a contributor release, an advisor agreement, a
translator agreement, a consent form, or a clearance. Names are never added from
memory at the end of a production, because the names most likely to be forgotten are
the ones with the least power to complain.

| | |
|---|---|
| Credit form | Each contributor chooses: named / anonymised / withheld. Their choice is recorded on the instrument, and it governs. |
| Spelling and diacritics | Exactly as the person writes it. Diacritics are correct in the credit even though they are forbidden in filenames. |
| Order and prominence | TBD — the studio's convention, applied consistently |
| Late changes | A person may change their credit choice up to publication. After publication, see the withdrawal terms on their instrument. |
