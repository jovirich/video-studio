# VO record sheet — TBD — production working title

> Copy this file; do not fill it in place.

Every proper noun and non-English term spoken in the production, with how it is
pronounced and who says so.

## Why this document exists

A VO session without this sheet is a session where the pronunciation of every name
in the episode is decided by whoever happens to be in the booth, at speed, from
spelling. The result is fixed at the mix stage or, more often, not at all — and a
mispronounced endonym is the single most reliable signal to the people the material
belongs to that nobody consulted them.

It is cheaper by two orders of magnitude to fill this in before the session.

The sheet is signed off as part of the **audio lock** gate. A proper noun that
appears in the narration and not on this sheet blocks that gate.

## How to fill each column

| Column | What a good entry looks like |
|---|---|
| **Term** | Exactly as it appears in the narration, with correct diacritics. If two forms appear, two rows. |
| **IPA** | Narrow enough to be actionable. Broad transcription that omits tone in a tonal language is not a pronunciation, it is a guess with symbols. |
| **Reference recording** | Asset store path to a recording by a speaker of the language. The recording is the authority; the IPA is the aide-memoire. A row with IPA and no recording is unverified. |
| **Language** | The language the form belongs to, not the language of the production. This is what tells the narrator which phonology applies. |
| **Verified by** | A named person who speaks the language and has heard the reference recording. Not a role, not a dictionary, not a model. |

The reference recording is deliberately the load-bearing field. IPA transmits
badly through a booth: it is read by someone who does not use it daily, under time
pressure, and small errors in it are invisible until playback.

## Terms with more than one accepted form

Where an endonym, an exonym, and a colonial form all exist, the production speaks
one of them and the choice is an editorial decision with a recorded reason. That
decision lives on the entity's record in the line
([../../records/_TEMPLATE_character.md](../../records/_TEMPLATE_character.md),
[../../records/_TEMPLATE_location.md](../../records/_TEMPLATE_location.md)), not
here. This sheet records only how the chosen form is said.
