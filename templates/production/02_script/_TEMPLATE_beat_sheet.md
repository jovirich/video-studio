# Beat sheet — TBD — production working title

> Copy this file; do not fill it in place.

One row per beat. A beat is the smallest unit that changes what the viewer
understands. If a row does not change anything, it is a transition, and transitions
are the shooting script's problem, not this document's.

## How to read the columns

| Column | Why it exists |
|---|---|
| **Beat** | A short handle. Used in cut notes and in the edit, so keep it stable once written. |
| **Turn** | What the viewer understands after this beat that they did not before. A beat with no turn is padding that will survive to the assembly because nothing flagged it. |
| **Rests on** | The claim IDs this beat asserts. A beat with no claims is doing rhetorical work only — legitimate, but worth knowing. |
| **Register** | The confidence the beat is spoken at. Must match the claims. This column is where a `probable` claim quietly becomes an established statement, so it is checked here rather than at fact-check. |
| **Picture** | What is on screen, in one phrase, and its provenance class. |
| **Gap** | The evidence this beat wants and does not have. Feeds the open questions register. |
| **Runtime** | Seconds. Sums to the section total in the outline. |
