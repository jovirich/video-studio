# Contributor release

> **STARTING POINT ONLY — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Must be reviewed by a qualified
> lawyer admitted in the relevant jurisdiction before use. Moral rights in
> particular cannot be waived at all in some jurisdictions and require specific
> wording in others. See [README.md](README.md).

## 4. AI and machine processing

The studio will use automated tools on the contribution for:

- [ ] TBD — restoration, upscaling, cleanup, transcription, translation drafting
- [ ] TBD — anything else, named specifically

The studio commits that the contribution **will not** be used to train, fine-tune,
or adapt any machine-learning model, by the studio or by any party the studio
provides it to.

TBD — where a third-party tool is used, name it and state its retention and training
terms as verified in the model terms register, on TBD — date. If those terms permit
training on inputs, the tool is not used on this material.

Restoration and cleanup **repair damage**. They do not invent content. Generating an
image, document, recording, or artefact intended or likely to be taken for a genuine
historical item is prohibited outright
([../../core/01_provenance_and_ai_disclosure.md](../../core/01_provenance_and_ai_disclosure.md) §2),
and "restoring" a photograph in a way that adds detail that was never there falls
inside that prohibition.

## 6. Moral rights

TBD — a lawyer must draft this clause for the relevant jurisdiction. In some,
attribution and integrity rights cannot be waived; in others a waiver requires
specific wording; in others still, the rights barely exist.

The studio's position regardless of what the law permits: the contributor is
attributed as they chose, and the contribution is not modified in a way that changes
its meaning without asking them.
