# Archive request letter

> **STARTING POINT ONLY — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Any permission the repository
> grants in reply is a licence, and the terms of that licence must be reviewed by
> a qualified lawyer before the material is used. See [README.md](README.md).

For approaching a repository, collection, museum, library, or private holder for
access to material or permission to reproduce it.

---

## Before writing

| | |
|---|---|
| What already exists | TBD — the catalogue entry, finding aid, or published reference. Asking for something already online is how a request gets deprioritised. |
| Who to write to | TBD — a named person where possible. "Dear Sir/Madam" to a general address is the slowest route into any institution. |
| Their published terms | TBD — most repositories publish a reproduction policy and a fee schedule. Read it first and ask inside its terms. |
| Lead time | TBD — assume months, not weeks, and plan the production around that rather than around hope. |
| Fees | TBD — budgeted before asking. A permission granted and then declined on cost wastes the repository's time and the studio's standing with them. |

subject rather than guessing at holdings — an archivist can help with the second and
not the first.

**What we would like to do.** TBD — choose and be specific:

- Consult the material on site
- Obtain a reproduction for research use only
- Reproduce the material in the production, for TBD — territories, media, term
- Both of the above

**How the material would be used.** TBD — where it would appear, at what length or
resolution, and in what context. State plainly whether the material would be altered
in any way, including cropping, grading, re-timing, or restoration.

**What we will not do.** We do not use generative tools to alter the content of
genuine archival material, and we do not present generated imagery as archival. Any
reconstruction in our work carries a persistent on-screen mark identifying it as
such, and a per-shot provenance record is published alongside the production.

**Credit.** We will reproduce your credit line exactly as you specify it. TBD — the
form you would like used.

**Access conditions.** We are able to work within conditions you place on the
material — restrictions on which items may be shown, on context, or on onward use.
Please tell us what applies. Where a condition would affect the work editorially, we
disclose it publicly rather than absorb it silently.

**Fees.** TBD — confirm that reproduction fees are budgeted, and ask for the current
schedule.

**Timing.** TBD — the date by which a decision would be useful, stated as
information rather than as pressure.

I would be glad to answer any questions, and to send a copy of the finished
production.
