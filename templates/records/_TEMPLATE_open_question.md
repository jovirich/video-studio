## Why open questions are registered rather than carried

An open question is a **publishable state**. "The record does not tell us" is a
finding about the archive, it belongs to the viewer, and it is frequently more
interesting than the hedge that would replace it.

An *unregistered* open question is a different thing. It lives in one person's head,
survives their week off, and gets resolved under deadline pressure by whoever needs
the sentence to end — which is precisely how a plausible placeholder enters a script
and then a film. Registering it converts a private uncertainty into a tracked one
with an owner and a resolution path.
