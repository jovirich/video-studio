## 3. Known starting points

| Source or lead | Where | Status |
|---|---|---|
| TBD | TBD — repository, catalogue, bibliography, person | TBD — not started / in progress / exhausted |

Leads from a generative tool are recorded here as **leads**, never as sources. Model
output is T5 — never citable — and every lead is verified against the actual document
before it becomes a claim. The specific failure this guards against: a fluent,
correctly formatted, entirely fictitious citation that survives because it looks
exactly like the real ones around it.
