The charter says what this studio is for. It does not restate
[core](../../../core/) or the studio's canon pack — a restatement drifts from the
original, and when the two disagree nobody knows which governs. It says only what is
true of **this studio and no other**.

communities whose history or work it uses — are frequently not its audience. Merging

it was about objected", which is not an answer.

## 8. Success conditions

**TBD — what would count as having succeeded, stated so that failing it is
recognisable.**

Audience numbers are the easy answer and rarely the real one. Better candidates: the
work is cited; a community it covered would work with the studio again; a correction
was issued promptly and the audience read it as a strength; a scholar who disagreed
with a production said it represented their position fairly.

**TBD — what would count as having failed while still looking successful.** This is
the more useful half, and the harder one to write while optimistic.

## 9. Corrections posture

**TBD — how this studio handles being wrong.**

Minimum: a public corrections log, published from the start rather than created
after the first error; a public intake address; corrections attached to the work
they correct, not filed where nobody looks; and a record that is never quietly
edited. See [corrections.md](corrections.md).

A studio that produces a corrections page only after being corrected has told its
audience something about how it expected this to go.
