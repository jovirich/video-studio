## What onboarding here is actually for

Most repositories onboard you to a codebase. This one is mostly *rules*, and the
rules exist because of specific failure modes that are not obvious until they happen.
The onboarding is therefore weighted toward **why a constraint exists**, because a
rule whose rationale is unknown becomes a rule people route around at 1am.

Three things a new contributor most often gets wrong, in order of frequency:

1. **Putting a change at the wrong layer.** The most common review correction. The
   placement test is in [first_week.md](first_week.md) day 2 and in
   [../../CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. **Filling in a template with something plausible.** Covered on day 1, and it is the
   one rule that is never negotiable.
3. **Claiming more maturity than exists** — writing "done" or a ✅ where the honest
   word is DESIGNED. Covered on day 1.
