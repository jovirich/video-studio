This is the gate that matters in this pack. It is the analogue of documentary's
fact-check, narrowed to the only claims that carry real exposure — and the exposure
is different in kind: a false product claim is a regulatory and contractual problem,
not only a credibility one.

The specific hazard of generative tooling here is that it makes it trivially easy to
show a UI, a result, or a use case that **does not exist** — a screen that was
rendered rather than captured, a metric invented to fill a slot in the edit.

## What this signature certifies

> Every assertion about the product — capability, performance, price, availability,
> comparison, and any figure or metric — is substantiated by a recorded evidence
> reference, and describes what **ships today** rather than what is planned. Roadmap
> language is flagged and approved separately.

## Checks

### Enumerate before you verify

- [ ] Every assertion about the product has been **listed**, from narration, on-screen text, captions, the description, the title, and the thumbnail. You cannot substantiate a claim you have not written down
- [ ] Each listed claim has an evidence reference recorded — a document, a test result, a pricing page, a support matrix — not a person's recollection
- [ ] Each evidence reference is dated, and the date is recent enough that the claim is still true

### Categories

- [ ] **Capability**: the product does the thing, in the shipping version, without a flag, a beta, or a manual step the film does not show
- [ ] **Performance**: every speed, throughput, accuracy, or uptime figure traces to a recorded measurement, with the conditions of that measurement stated where they materially affect the number
- [ ] **Price**: matches current published pricing, including currency, tier, and what is excluded
- [ ] **Availability**: territories, platforms, and plan tiers are accurate as of the delivery date
- [ ] **Comparison**: any comparison against a named competitor is escalated, not checked here — comparative advertising carries jurisdiction-specific rules this pack does not contain
- [ ] **Figures and metrics**: no number appears that was chosen because it fit the edit. Every one has a source

### Ships today

- [ ] Every claim describes what ships **today**, in the version generally available on the delivery date
- [ ] Roadmap language is identified explicitly and approved separately, in writing. "Coming soon" is a commitment someone has to keep
- [ ] Nothing shown behind a feature flag, in an internal build, or in a staging environment is presented as generally available
- [ ] Where availability differs by tier or territory, the film says so or does not assert it

### Depiction of the product

- [ ] **Every product-interface shot is captured, not generated.** A rendered UI is this pack's equivalent of a fabricated archival photograph
- [ ] Screen recordings show the shipping build, not a mockup, a prototype, or a design file
- [ ] Where speed is compressed for the edit, the compression is disclosed on screen rather than implied as real-time
- [ ] Results shown on screen are real outputs of the product, not composed for the frame
- [ ] **No generated customer testimonial.** Synthesising a person who appears to endorse the product is fabricated evidence under core, whatever the marketing convention
- [ ] Real testimonials carry a release, and the person said what the cut has them saying — including after the cut

### The claims that are not about the product

- [ ] Any assertion about history, economics, society, or a market — as opposed to the product — has been identified. Those claims need documentary-history's evidence chain, not this pack's, and are escalated rather than waved through
- [ ] Disclaimers, where used, are legible at the size and duration they appear, and correct what they claim to correct

## Do not sign if

- **A claim's evidence is "product told me".** An evidence reference is a document you
  can open on the delivery date, not a conversation.
- **A capability is shown that requires a flag, a beta, or a step the film omits.**
  The film is the claim; the caveat that was in the briefing document is not.
- **Any interface shot was generated.** Not "upscaled", not "cleaned up", not
  "recreated because the capture was low resolution". Recapture it.
- **A figure has no measurement behind it**, or the measurement conditions are
  material and unstated.
- **Roadmap language survived without separate written approval.**
- **A comparison against a named competitor is in the cut.** Escalate. This pack does
  not cover it and a checklist cannot make it safe.
- **You are told the launch date makes this impossible.** The launch date is exactly
  why this gate blocks delivery.
- **You signed another gate on this production.**
